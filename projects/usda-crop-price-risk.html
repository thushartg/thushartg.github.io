Crop Price & Risk

About

Fourteen years of monthly USDA NASS farm-gate commodity prices joined with FRED consumer price indices across 10,000+ observations to measure volatility, state exposure, and 6-month Prophet horizons.

Co-Movement

Joining state-level farm-gate prices with FRED commodity CPI series showed tight co-movement across wheat, corn, and soybeans (r = 0.90–0.96), with corn exhibiting the highest coefficient of variation at 28.5%.

Forecasting

Seasonal Prophet fits with 90% uncertainty intervals projected a +17% 6-month recovery for soybeans from late-2024 lows while corn and wheat held near support.
