CleanUNet

About

Time-domain 1D U-Net denoising autoencoder built in PyTorch and trained on the Microsoft Deep Noise Suppression (DNS) Challenge corpus for speech enhancement.

Why Time-Domain

Spectrogram-masking models discard phase or reuse noisy input phase during inverse STFT reconstruction, producing metallic artifacts under loud non-stationary noise.

Loss Design

Combining waveform L1 error with multi-resolution STFT spectral convergence preserves crisp consonant transients without phase estimation drift.
