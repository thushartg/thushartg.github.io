TasteNet

About

Restaurant discovery web app built in Buffalo that replaces flat five-star averages with reviews weighted by cosine similarity against your own palate.

Challenge

Standard review platforms collapse opposite diners into one average: a 4.2-star score tells you little if the reviewers dislike chili heat and you actively seek it out.

5D Palate Vector

Onboarding scores each diner across five axes (spice tolerance, sweetness, saltiness, adventurousness, regional authenticity) in PostgreSQL via Supabase and weights restaurant scores by reader–reviewer cosine similarity.
