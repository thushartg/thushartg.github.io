Claims Pipeline

About

End-to-end ETL over 150,000 medical claims ($498.6M paid) across HDFS, PySpark, Hive, and Snowflake, paired with an in-browser DuckDB-WASM SQL console.

Challenge

Raw flat CSVs arrived with mixed casing on status enums, skewed provider billing distributions, and inpatient/outpatient stays mixed in one table—causing naive SQL queries to misstate readmission denominators.

Reconciliation

Normalizing string enums during the PySpark Parquet write and restricting 30-day readmission denominators to inpatient stays aligned Spark, Hive, and Snowflake at 10.16% readmissions and 7.96% denials (11,940 claims).
