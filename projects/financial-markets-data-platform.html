Markets Platform

About

Containerized weekday ELT ingesting daily equity bars from Yahoo Finance and macroeconomic series from FRED into Google BigQuery, modeled in dbt and scheduled via Apache Airflow.

Grain Alignment

Joining daily equity bars directly with monthly and quarterly FRED releases breaks BI aggregations. Separate BigQuery staging views and explicit forward-fill logic inside a dedicated dbt mart keep trading-day calendars consistent.

Quality Gates

Seven dbt schema, uniqueness, and non-null checks gate downstream publication so Looker Studio never refreshes on partial or duplicate pulls.
