Emoji-GAN

About

Deep Convolutional Generative Adversarial Network (DCGAN) in PyTorch synthesizing 64×64 RGB emoji icons from Gaussian latent vectors.

Challenge

Unlike natural photographs, emoji sprites have crisp flat-color boundaries and strict circular symmetry, making checkerboard upsampling artifacts or mode collapse immediately visible.

Stabilization

Compositing transparent channels prior to [-1, 1] tanh normalization and pairing strided LeakyReLU discriminator stages with calibrated Adam momentum stabilized facial synthesis.

PyTorch, Torchvision, NumPy
