MacControl

About

Low-latency touchscreen and trackpad control of your Mac from an iPhone, built on ScreenCaptureKit, VideoToolbox H.264/HEVC, CoreGraphics HID injection, and CryptoKit end-to-end encryption.

Security Model

Ephemeral X25519 + 6-digit PIN (HKDF-SHA256) key agreement encrypts every video, audio, clipboard, and pointer frame with ChaCha20-Poly1305—even when routed across the zero-knowledge WebSocket relay.

Wire Protocol

Custom binary packet framing over NWConnection with TCP_NODELAY eliminates base64 overhead over local Wi-Fi, USB, or Tailscale.
